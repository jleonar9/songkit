🎶 I built something for musicians and church worship teams, and it's free.

When I finished my song "Gold Dust Feet," I realized releasing it meant a whole list of extra work: synced lyrics for Spotify, lyric videos for Reels and Shorts, a Spotify Canvas loop, plus slides and lyric sheets for church. Most tools for that cost money or make you upload your unreleased music to someone else's server.

So I made Songkit. 👇

✅ Times your lyrics to your vocal automatically (and you can fix any line)
✅ Makes vertical lyric videos with captions
✅ Creates a Spotify Canvas loop
✅ Builds ProPresenter slides and a printable lyric sheet
🔒 Runs on YOUR computer, so your music never gets uploaded
💸 100% free and open source

The video below was made start to finish with Songkit. 🔥

Download it here (Windows): https://songkit-solidground.netlify.app
Source code: https://github.com/jleonar9/songkit

If you lead worship, make music, or run media at your church, give it a try and tell me what you think. Share it with someone who could use it! 🙏
