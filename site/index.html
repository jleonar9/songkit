Help

Quick answers.

The Launch button doesn't do anything

Install Songkit first (steps above). If it's installed, double-click the Songkit icon on your desktop instead. The page connects on its own once Songkit is running.

It says “Running” but the app area stays blank

Some browsers won't show local apps inside a web page. Click Open in its own window; it's the same app.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. This website only shows the app. Songkit itself runs on your PC, and your files are saved in Documents\Songkit.

Do I need a gaming graphics card?

No. Songkit works on any Windows PC. An NVIDIA card just makes lyric timing faster. On a laptop, choose the medium or small model.

The black Songkit window opened. Can I close it?

Keep it open while you use Songkit. Closing it quits the app.

Mac or Linux?

Yes, with a few manual steps. See the instructions on GitHub.

How do I uninstall?

Start menu → Songkit → Uninstall Songkit. Your songs in Documents\Songkit are kept.

AI-made songs

If AI made part of your song, say so where your distributor asks (DistroKid calls this AI Credits). Songkit makes release assets; it doesn't hide how a song was made.
